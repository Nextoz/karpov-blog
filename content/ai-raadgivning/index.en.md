Many companies have tried a chatbot and got no further. I am a freelance AI consultant in Copenhagen, and I help small businesses find the tasks where AI actually saves time, and I build the solution so it still works three months from now.

I work in English and Danish, in Copenhagen or remote.

## What I can help with

- Finding the two or three workflows where AI gives the most for the money
- Building an automation for things like email, documents, reports or research
- Choosing a model and a provider, including European hosting if your data has to stay in the EU
- Setting clear limits for what the AI may do on its own and what a person has to approve
- Teaching your team to use the solution and maintain it without me

## How it works

1. **A free 20-minute call.** You tell me what takes time in your business, and I tell you honestly whether AI is the answer.
2. **Assessment.** I look at your workflows and data and write a recommendation you can use whether or not you continue with me.
3. **Build.** I build one thing at a time and show it to you along the way.
4. **Handover.** You get documentation and a walkthrough, so the solution does not depend on me.

## Why me

- **I use what I build.** My [scheduled agents](/en/projects/#scouts) run every morning, and I built my own phone app with a team of AI agents that I direct and review.
- **I know the difference between a demo and operations.** I have more than six years of experience with software development, DevOps and systems that have to run every day. An automation that fails silently is worse than no automation.
- **I am not tied to one vendor.** I have run the same tasks on American, Chinese and European models, including with a European cloud provider, and I choose by the task, the price and where the data ends up.
- **Security is there from the start.** Read [how I work with security](/en/projects/#security) in my own agent systems.
- **I already work directly with a local business.** See [Vintønden](/en/projects/#vintonden) in my portfolio.
